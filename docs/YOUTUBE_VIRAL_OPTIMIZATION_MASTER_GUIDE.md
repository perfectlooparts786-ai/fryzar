## 5. Thumbnail Design Rules for 10%+ CTR

1. **Rule of 3 Words:** Never use more than 3 bold words on a thumbnail (e.g., "STOP DOING THIS", "IT'S FINALLY HERE", "SECRET REVEALED").
2. **High Color Contrast:** Dark background (#0F0F0F) + Neon Yellow (#FFD700) or Neon Green (#00FF66) text with black drop-shadow.
3. **Focal Element:** One big face (expressing shock/excitement) or one big central icon/logo occupying 40% of the image.
4. **Resolution:** 1280x720 PNG, optimized for mobile screens (over 80% of views come from mobile).
